Diagnosis: Type 2 diabetes with high blood sugar (hyperglycemia) and a urinary tract infection.

WHAT HAPPENED
Your blood sugar was very high when you arrived and you had a urine infection. We gave you fluids, insulin, and antibiotics. Your sugar is now in a safer range.

MEDICATIONS
Metformin 500 mg tablet: take 1 tablet by mouth twice daily with meals.
Glargine insulin: inject 20 units under the skin once daily at bedtime. Check your blood sugar before each dose. If your sugar is below 100, call the clinic before taking your insulin.
Ciprofloxacin 500 mg tablet: take 1 tablet by mouth every 12 hours for 3 days. Do not take it within 2 hours of antacids or dairy products.
Glucose tablets: chew 4 tablets if your blood sugar is below 70.
Take Metformin 500 mg tablet twice daily with meals even on days you eat less.

HOME CARE
Check your blood sugar 4 times a day: before meals and at bedtime. Write down every result. Drink water throughout the day. Choose foods with fewer sugars and starches, and eat meals at regular times.

CALL YOUR DOCTOR IF
Your blood sugar is over 300 two times in a row, you have burning with urination that is not better in 2 days, or you have a fever over 100.4 degrees F.

GO TO THE EMERGENCY ROOM OR CALL 911 IF
You are very sleepy or confused, you have fruity-smelling breath with nausea and vomiting, you pass out, or your blood sugar stays below 70 after you have treated it twice.

Diabetes education class: the clinic will call you with a date.
