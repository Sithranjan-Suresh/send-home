Principal diagnosis: Acute on chronic systolic congestive heart failure (decompensated), volume overload.

HOSPITAL COURSE
The patient presented with bilateral lower extremity edema, interval weight gain, and orthopnea. Intravenous loop diuretic therapy resulted in net diuresis of approximately 6 pounds. Serial troponin assays and electrocardiography showed no evidence of acute myocardial infarction. The patient is euvolemic and hemodynamically stable at discharge.

DISCHARGE MEDICATIONS
1. Furosemide 40 mg tablet: take 1 tablet by mouth every morning (loop diuretic).
2. Lisinopril 10 mg tablet: take 1 tablet by mouth once daily. Renal function and serum potassium will be reassessed at your first visit.
3. Metoprolol succinate: take by mouth once daily. Dose titration will be determined at your first clinic visit.
4. Potassium chloride 20 mEq: take 1 tablet by mouth once daily with food.
MEDICATION CHANGES: Discontinue ibuprofen and naproxen. Nonsteroidal anti-inflammatory drugs promote sodium and fluid retention and may precipitate decompensation.

SELF-MONITORING AND DIETARY RESTRICTIONS
Obtain a daily weight each morning after voiding and before breakfast, and record it in a log. Adhere to a sodium restriction of less than 2,000 mg per day. Fluid intake is limited to 64 ounces (approximately 8 cups) per day. Elevate the lower extremities when seated. Engage in light ambulation daily and rest when fatigued.

NOTIFY YOUR CARE TEAM IF
You gain more than 3 pounds in one day or 5 pounds in one week; if your legs, ankles, or abdomen swell more than usual; if you need more pillows to sleep; if you feel dizzy when you stand; or if you have a dry cough that will not go away.

ACTIVATE 911 OR PROCEED TO THE EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT FOR
Chest pain or pressure, severe shortness of breath at rest, fainting, rapid or irregular palpitations, or coughing up pink, foamy sputum.

FOLLOW-UP
Heart failure clinic with Dr. Okafor within 7 days; bring your weight log and all medication bottles.
Blood tests (basic metabolic panel for kidney function and potassium) will be obtained at the clinic visit.
Cardiology visit in 4 weeks for reassessment of ejection fraction and guideline-directed therapy.
