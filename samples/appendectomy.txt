WOUND CARE
You may shower after 48 hours, allowing soap and water to run over the incisions, then pat dry. Do not submerge the incisions in a bathtub, hot tub, or pool for 2 weeks. Do not pull off the skin adhesive or the adhesive strips; they will detach spontaneously within 1 to 2 weeks.

ACTIVITY RESTRICTIONS
No lifting greater than 10 pounds for 4 weeks. Ambulate daily. Sedentary work may resume in approximately 1 week. Refrain from athletic activity until cleared by your surgeon.

CONTACT YOUR SURGEON IF
You develop a fever of 100.4 degrees F or higher, redness or swelling around an incision, purulent drainage, intractable vomiting, or pain that worsens rather than improves. Also call if you have not had a bowel movement in 3 days.

PROCEED TO THE EMERGENCY DEPARTMENT FOR
Severe belly pain, a rigid distended abdomen, chest pain, trouble breathing, or a painful swollen calf (possible deep vein thrombosis).
