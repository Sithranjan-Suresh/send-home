PAIN AND OTHER MEDICINES

WOUND CARE
You may shower after 48 hours. Let soap and water run over the cuts and pat them dry. Do not soak in a bathtub, hot tub, or pool for 2 weeks. Do not pull off the skin glue or the strips; they will fall off on their own in 1 to 2 weeks.

ACTIVITY

CALL YOUR SURGEON IF
You have a fever of 100.4 degrees F or higher, redness or swelling around a cut, drainage that looks like pus, vomiting that does not stop, or pain that gets worse instead of better. Call also if you have not had a bowel movement in 3 days.

GO TO THE EMERGENCY ROOM IF
You have severe belly pain, a hard swollen belly, chest pain, trouble breathing, or a painful swollen calf.
