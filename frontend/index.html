How we check
  • The AI must copy a word-for-word quote from your paperwork for every line. Code, not the AI, looks for that quote in the original.
  • If a quote can't be found, or a dose is missing, the line is flagged. Nothing is guessed.
  • Questions are answered only from your paperwork. If the sheet doesn't say, the app says so.

Official medicine labels are fetched from MedlinePlus with Anakin. Only the medicine name is sent.
